About Me

Robotics

Landslide Detection Drone — Built From Scratch

Beyond the tennis court, I channel my curiosity into engineering. I designed and built a fully functional drone with vibration and soil moisture from the ground up — from selecting components and wiring circuits to programming the flight controller.

This project was part of a greater vision to explore how technology can solve real-world problems like warning a community of impending landslide disasters. Every challenge in the build — balancing propellers, calibrating sensors with the drone code, debugging code — taught me problem-solving skills that go far beyond the workshop.
